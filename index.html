action-ring-home-client

action-ring-home-client is the Google sign-in client for action-ring, a personal home-automation project. It turns an ActionRing N3 (Stream Dock N3) control pad into a remote for one living room's TV, sound bar and streaming box, using Home Assistant running on a Raspberry Pi in the same home.

The control pad's keys and knobs switch the TV and sound modes, move through streaming apps, skip video chapters and mute ad breaks. When a feature needs a Google service, the owner signs in once with their Google account on a TV-style sign-in screen. The app then uses that access only for the feature it was granted for.

This is not a commercial product or a public service. It is used only by the project's owner and the people in their household.
